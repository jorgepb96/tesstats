1. Independent, unofficial project

Tesstats is an independent, community-built app. It is not part of TeslaMate, is not a TeslaMate client published by the TeslaMate project, and has no relationship of any kind with TeslaMate, its maintainers, or its contributors.

Tesstats contains no TeslaMate or TeslaMateApi code. It only reads data made available by compatible services that a user chooses to operate. The TeslaMate team does not review, support, or answer questions about Tesstats.

3. Choose the right support channel

For Tesla users

Questions about a Tesla account, vehicle operation, charging network, service, warranty, safety alert, or the official Tesla app belong with Tesla Support.

For TeslaMate users

Questions about installing, updating, securing, or repairing a TeslaMate server belong in the TeslaMate documentation, the TeslaMate project, or its community support spaces.

For TeslaMateApi users

Questions about that API belong in the TeslaMateApi project.

For Tesstats users

Questions about the Tesstats interface, connection fields, exports, widgets, or Driver Display belong in the Tesstats issue tracker on GitHub.

Tesstats maintainers cannot see or repair your Tesla account, TeslaMate server, Apple account, TestFlight account, or vehicle.

4. Safety and vehicle control

Tesstats is read only and does not send vehicle control commands. Driver Display is supplemental. It is not an official instrument panel, driver assistance system, emergency service, or turn by turn navigation system.

Do not interact with the app while driving. Follow the vehicle display, road signs, local law, and official safety guidance.

5. Accuracy and estimates

Range, battery health, cost, savings, carbon, speed, route, and consumption values can include estimates. Source data can be late or incomplete. Verify any value before using it for safety, maintenance, payment, tax, reimbursement, or legal purposes.
